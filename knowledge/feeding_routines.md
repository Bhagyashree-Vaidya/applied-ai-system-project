title: Feeding routines in multi-pet homes
---
## Consistent meal times
Feeding at consistent times each day helps digestion, makes it easier to notice appetite changes, and makes medication timing easier.

## Dogs and cats at mealtime
Most adult dogs do well on two meals a day. Cats often prefer several small meals. In homes with both, feed pets separately or at slightly different times so the dog cannot eat the cat's food; cat food is too rich for dogs and cats can be stressed by a dog nearby.

## Combining walks and meals
A common routine is a calm morning walk first, then breakfast after the dog has settled. Feeding the cat while the dog is out on the walk is an easy way to keep meals separate.

## Appetite changes
A pet that skips more than one meal, or eats much more than usual, should be watched closely; a cat that has not eaten for more than a day, or a rabbit that stops eating, needs a vet.
