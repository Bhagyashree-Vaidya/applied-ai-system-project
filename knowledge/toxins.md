title: Common household toxins
---
## Dogs
Chocolate, grapes and raisins, xylitol (a sweetener in some gum, peanut butter and baked goods), onions and garlic, and macadamia nuts are toxic to dogs. Grapes and raisins can cause kidney failure even in small amounts.

## Cats
All parts of lilies, including pollen and the water in the vase, can cause fatal kidney failure in cats. Human pain medications such as acetaminophen and ibuprofen are dangerous for cats.

## Rabbits
Avocado, chocolate, onions and many houseplants are toxic to rabbits.

## What to do
If a pet may have eaten something toxic, contact a veterinarian or a pet poison hotline immediately, even if the pet seems fine. Do not try to make the pet vomit unless a vet tells you to.
