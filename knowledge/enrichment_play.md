title: Play and enrichment
---
## Cat play sessions
Cats do best with two or three short play sessions of 10-15 minutes a day using wand toys that mimic prey. Ending a play session with a small meal or treat follows the natural hunt-eat-groom-sleep cycle.

## Dog enrichment
Puzzle feeders, snuffle mats, scent games and short training sessions give dogs mental exercise. They are useful on days with less time for long walks.

## Senior and low-energy pets
Older pets still need daily play, just shorter and gentler. Several short sessions are better than one long one.
