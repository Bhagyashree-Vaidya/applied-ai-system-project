title: Dog exercise and walks
---
## Daily exercise needs
Most healthy adult dogs need between 30 minutes and 2 hours of physical activity a day, depending on breed, age and energy level. Young, high-energy dogs usually sit at the top of that range. Splitting activity into two or more walks or play sessions is easier on joints than one long outing.

## Walk timing and meals
Avoid vigorous exercise for about an hour before and after a large meal, especially for large, deep-chested breeds, because it is linked to a higher risk of bloat (gastric dilatation-volvulus). A calm sniff walk is fine; save fetch and running for later.

## Sniff walks and mental exercise
Letting a dog sniff during walks is mental enrichment. A slower 20-minute sniff walk can tire a dog as much as a faster, longer walk. Puzzle feeders and short training sessions (5-10 minutes) also count toward daily activity.

## Hot weather and paws
In hot weather, walk early in the morning or in the evening. If pavement is too hot to hold the back of your hand on it for 7 seconds, it is too hot for paws.

## Limping
A dog that is limping should rest and avoid running or jumping. If the limp lasts more than a day, is severe, or the leg is swollen or the dog will not put weight on it, contact a veterinarian.
