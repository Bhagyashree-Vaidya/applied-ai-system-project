## Never guess a dose
Medication doses for pets depend on species, weight, age, kidney and liver function, and other medications. Only give the dose your veterinarian prescribed, and never give human medications unless a vet tells you to.

## Routines and reminders
Give medications at the same times every day and record each dose. Recurring reminders in a schedule help multi-pet households avoid missed or double doses.

## Missed doses
If you miss a dose, ask your vet or pharmacist what to do instead of doubling the next dose.
